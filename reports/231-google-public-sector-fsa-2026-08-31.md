## C) Level and Strategy

**The clearance is an absolute hard block.**

TS/SCI (Top Secret / Sensitive Compartmented Information) is a US government security clearance that requires:
1. US citizenship — **F-1 OPT holders are not US citizens**
2. Extensive background investigation (7–15 years personal history, foreign contacts, finances)
3. Typically sponsored by a cleared contractor or federal agency

F-1 visa holders cannot obtain TS/SCI clearance. Period. The minimum qualification reads "Active, or the **ability to obtain**, Top Secret/SCI security clearance" — but the ability to obtain is defined by citizenship status. This is not a "will sponsor" question; it is a citizenship gate.

**Why the score isn't lower (1.5 vs 1.0):**

The role archetype is genuinely one of Parimala's best targets — Field Solutions Architect / Forward Deployed Engineer doing rapid GenAI prototyping for real customers is exactly the pattern where her combination of domain expertise + hands-on AI (LeaseI, AgentProbe) + communication skills shines. If this same role existed without the clearance requirement (e.g., Google Cloud commercial FSA, not Public Sector), it would score 3.8–4.2. The 1.5 preserves that signal without implying it's worth pursuing.

**Closest alternative:** Look for Google Cloud Field Solutions Architect or Customer Engineer (AI/ML) roles in the commercial sector (not Public Sector). Same company, same tools, same GenAI rapid prototyping mission — no clearance requirement.
