## B) Match with CV

**Hard Block — Security Clearance:**

DoD security clearances (Secret and above) require US citizenship or Lawful Permanent Resident status in most cases. H-1B holders on F-1 STEM OPT transitioning to H-1B are non-immigrant visa holders and are generally ineligible. "Ability to obtain one" does not help — the disqualifier is immigration status, not willingness. This cannot be resolved by the employer or candidate.
