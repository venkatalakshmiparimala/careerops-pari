**Public Trust note:** Public Trust (NACI/MBI) is NOT a security clearance. It is a federal suitability investigation. Non-citizens including OPT holders can and do obtain Public Trust, particularly for NIH contractor positions. The process typically takes 2-4 months and requires residency history, background check, and references. It is not the same as SECRET/TS and does not require US citizenship. Flag proactively at recruiter screen: "I am on F-1 STEM OPT -- I understand Public Trust investigations apply to non-citizens and am prepared to complete that process."
