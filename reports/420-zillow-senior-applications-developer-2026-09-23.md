## C) Why SKIP

**Anaplan is the primary job.** The first and most prominent responsibility is "Own Anaplan system architecture for sales incentive compensation, including model design, calculation logic, territory hierarchies, and plan administration across all business units." Anaplan is a specialized connected planning platform. Ownership means being the team's subject matter expert, not a user. This is a zero in the portfolio with no adjacent proof point. Candidates without Anaplan experience will struggle to pass the screening bar for a role where it is the headline responsibility.

**Databricks DABs are "required."** The JD explicitly marks Databricks Asset Bundles (DABs) for CI/CD deployment as "required" — not a nice-to-have. Databricks is not in the portfolio at all. The broader Databricks data warehouse design responsibility compounds this — it's not just tooling familiarity, it's architectural ownership.

**SOX compliance is a hard requirement.** "Maintain data pipelines and integration workflows in compliance with SOX audit controls, ensuring traceability, access governance, and change management standards." SOX is a financial audit compliance framework. Parimala has HIPAA depth (healthcare PHI), which is strong but completely different. SOX knowledge is not transferable from HIPAA.

**Archetype mismatch is the root issue.** This is a finance operations data engineering role whose primary purpose is managing variable compensation infrastructure. The AI component is a small slice: "Build and expand the team's AI technical stack, including integrating the codebase with Claude Code." That is a single bullet among many. This is not FDE, not Applied AI, not ML Engineering. It is internal tooling for sales comp — a domain Parimala has no background in.

**What's present is real but not enough.** Python 9yr, data pipelines, SQL optimization, CI/CD, API integration — all genuine. But these are table stakes for this role, not differentiators. The differentiating requirements are the ones that are absent: Anaplan, Databricks, SOX, Salesforce/Workday/Zuora.
