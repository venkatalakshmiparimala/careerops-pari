## C) Scoring

**FDE archetype is profile priority #1.** This is the cleanest archetype match in the pipeline after Parakeet Health. The role description -- "real code, real environments, measurable business impact" -- is exactly the framing the profile targets. Magizhs is the direct customer delivery proof point the JD calls out as a hard requirement ("consulting, professional services, or forward deployed engineering -- this is a requirement, not a nice-to-have").

**H-1B is as safe as it gets.** Salesforce filed 1,848 H-1B LCAs in 2025 with 99% approval. "Agentforce Forward Deployed Engineer" is a named H-1B job category at Salesforce -- they have sponsored this specific title. No need to ask; it's confirmed infrastructure.

**All required technical skills are met.** Python, JS/TS, LLMs, prompt engineering, customer delivery, engineering rigor for AI outputs, data modeling + APIs. The JD says "fluent in at least one of Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, Java, or Apex" -- Python 9yr covers it completely.

**Salesforce platform gap is real but managed.** "Salesforce platform experience or certifications" is listed as a nice-to-have, not a requirement. The actual required skills are language-agnostic engineering + LLM/agent expertise + customer delivery. Salesforce trains FDEs on the Agentforce platform -- the expectation is not that candidates arrive with SFDC expertise, but that they can learn it. LangGraph experience transfers to Agentforce agent patterns. However: the role involves building on the Agentforce platform daily, so this gap will be visible in technical screens. Worth mentioning proactively in the cover letter.

**LangChain nice-to-have: confirmed hit.** The JD lists "Familiarity with agent frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex)" as a nice-to-have. LangChain and LangGraph are the core stack -- AgentProbe and LeaseI both use LangGraph (the graph-based LangChain extension).

**Snowflake / Databricks absent.** Listed as nice-to-have. AWS Glue + PostgreSQL covers the data pipeline dimension; the specific warehouse tools are learnable.

**Senior FDE level likely.** With 9yr experience, Salesforce will evaluate at Senior FDE band. The profile's hard block on "Senior at formal-leveling companies" applies to SWE/MLE/SA tracks where prior Senior title is required at the interview stage. FDE is a different ladder -- customer delivery + AI skills + engineering judgment are the evaluation axes, not title-matching. The confirmed rejection pattern (AppFolio, Amazon, Juniper Square) was on engineering leveling, not delivery/consulting tracks. Proceed.

**Comp is exceptional.** $88,970–$287,910 base range (multi-level, multi-location). At Senior FDE in a standard US location: est. $150–$200K base. CA/NY/select metro: $180–$230K. Plus Salesforce equity (meaningful at this scale), bonuses, and full benefits. Well above target range.

- LangChain/LangGraph (nice-to-have hit)
