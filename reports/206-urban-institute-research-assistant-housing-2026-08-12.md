No policy research candidate will have that operational depth. The domain match is not theoretical — it is exceptional.

**But the role is wrong for the domain fit:** Research Assistants at Urban Institute use R/Stata/SAS to run regressions and write policy briefs. Parimala's value — 8yr production engineering, LangGraph agentic systems, AI eval harnesses — is entirely inaccessible to this role. She'd be doing statistical analysis in R for $52K, not building the AI-powered tools that would make this research ten times faster.

**The right move for housing domain expertise:** If Urban Institute is the target, look for a **Senior Research Associate**, **Data Engineer**, or **AI/Technology** role within their organization — not Research Assistant.
