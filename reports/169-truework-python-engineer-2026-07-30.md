### STAR+R stories to prepare

#### [Domain] Catching Income Fraud Manually at Scale
**S:** Entrata processed affordable housing certifications for 100K+ MAU — income fraud (fake paystubs, misrepresented income, household-composition fraud) was a pervasive problem in LIHTC/HCV/HUD portfolios.
**T:** Build compliance automation that catches what manual review misses, and that meets federal/state regulatory standards.
**A:** Built Section 8/HCV, LIHTC, and HUD compliance modules (Python, PHP, PostgreSQL) that automated income eligibility verification and AMI threshold calculations. Applied analytical hypothesis testing on consumer income data to flag anomalies. Ran recertification workflows with deterministic compliance checks.
**R:** Zero compliance violations across all client environments. Entrata published industry research on fake paystub detection in the sector — the manual work I was doing became the basis for that research.
**Reflection:** Fraud in regulated housing is structurally different from fraud in fintech — it's lower-tech but higher-stakes (eviction, loss of housing subsidy). The detection rules need to be deterministic and auditable, not just probabilistic.
**Best for:** "Tell me about a complex domain problem you solved," "Why Truework?", "What makes you different from other candidates?"

#### [Production Reliability] Entrata 99.5% Uptime
**S:** Shipping 20+ full-stack modules across 120+ Agile sprints for 100K+ MAU with a small team — production reliability was non-negotiable.
**T:** Maintain 99.5% uptime while shipping at pace, debugging fast when issues arose.
**A:** Built internal debugging and performance tools that cut debugging time by 40%; optimized 200+ SQL queries (30% performance gain); maintained a bug count below 10 for 6+ consecutive months; used Docker containerization to cut microservices execution time by 50%.
**R:** 99.5% uptime sustained over 6+ consecutive months; management appreciation recognition for engineering quality.
**Reflection:** Production reliability is about systems, not heroics. Monitoring, good tooling, and fast-path debugging matter more than individual brilliance during incidents.
**Best for:** "Tell me about a high-stakes production situation," "How do you ensure reliability?", "What's your approach to owned services?"

#### [Compliance on Sensitive Data] AWS S3 Migration + PHI Encryption
**S:** Needed to migrate all resident sensitive data (SSNs, phone numbers) to AWS S3 while maintaining HIPAA/PHI compliance — failure would mean zero compliance for all client environments.
**T:** Migrate 100% of resident sensitive data with zero security incidents; update 50%+ of product codebase for encryption.
**A:** Used Libsodium encryption on all data in transit and at rest; updated every insertion and retrieval path in the codebase; validated encryption compliance end-to-end before migration.
**R:** 100% PHI compliance achieved; zero security incidents; entire migration stabilized in production.
**Reflection:** On migrations, encryption compliance has to be baked into the pipeline, not verified after. Every touch point is a risk surface.
**Best for:** "Tell me about working with sensitive data," "How do you approach security in production systems?"
