## C) Level and Strategy

**Level:** Senior BI (6+ years). Experience level is fine — gap is specifically the Tableau depth.

**Honest framing:**
- Lead with SQL depth (8yr production, 200+ queries, 30% gain) + Python automation (80% workload eliminated) — these are concrete
- The AI agent preferred requirement is the differentiator: "I can build the automation that replaces the manual BI work, not just do the BI work"
- Snowflake/SAP HANA: "My production SQL is PostgreSQL + Oracle — Snowflake syntax is standard SQL with minor differences. I'll be at full speed within a week."
- Tableau gap is the hard one — acknowledge it, point to GMU dashboards as proof of visualization skill, emphasize that Tableau is a tool, not a discipline

**If they flag Tableau gap:** "My visualization experience includes Tableau, ggplot2, Quarto, and Power BI — I've built dashboards for faculty leadership and stakeholder reporting. The 5-year mark is specifically in Tableau; the data storytelling and dashboard design skills are 8 years."
