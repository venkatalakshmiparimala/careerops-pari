1. **Marketing analytics domain — no experience.** The Subscriptions & Customer Growth team is optimizing Google One, YouTube Premium, and consumer app growth. 2. **No A/B testing or causal inference in production.** The JD explicitly calls out "controlled experiment design and causal inference methods" as preferred and "design and analyze controlled experiments or counterfactual causal inference studies" as a core responsibility. This is the central skill of a marketing analytics role. No proof points — only statistical coursework.

CrashClarity is a classification/regression model on accident records — not segmentation for marketing purposes. LeaseI is a compliance RAG pipeline. No segmentation or personalization proof point exists.
