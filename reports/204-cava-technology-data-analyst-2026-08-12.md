## C) Level and Strategy

**Level:** Mid-level DA. 2–5 years required. Parimala has 8 years of engineering + MS Analytics — overqualified, but this is a manageable gap (unlike the entry-level TeamBuilderz role). CAVA's tech team is likely sophisticated enough to appreciate production engineering depth.

**The DC angle is real:** CAVA's DC office is at 14 Ridge Square NW — commutable from Virginia without relocation. That's a genuine practical positive.

**The visa reality check:** CAVA explicitly states no sponsorship. On F-1 STEM OPT (3-year authorization from May 2026 graduation), Parimala can legally work there until ~May 2029. But:
- The H-1B lottery is in April each year
- To work past OPT expiry, she needs an employer to file H-1B
- If CAVA won't sponsor, she'd need to leave the company or find a workaround (e.g., changing employers while keeping the role, which isn't possible)
- Result: a hard employment cliff in ~3 years

This is categorically different from roles at top H-1B sponsors (Amazon, Google, Microsoft, LangChain) where the path is clear.

**Verdict:** The role is a genuine match on skills, location, and comp. The sponsorship issue is the single decisive factor. Apply only with eyes open: this is a 3-year engagement with no renewal path at CAVA.
